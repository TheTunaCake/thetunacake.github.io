AmiiNest

NFC Write & Collect

AmiiNest is an upcoming iPhone app for amiibo collectors. It brings a searchable catalogue, personal collection tracking, and compatible NFC tag tools together in one place.

Browse & Search

Explore a searchable catalogue of figures and characters, organized into series.

Manage Your Collection

Keep track of owned items, favorites, wishlists, and missing collectibles.

Import Your Data

Use compatible, user-provided files to prepare information for supported NFC workflows.

NFC Tag Writing

Write compatible NFC tags with your iPhone using data you are authorized to use.

AmiiNest is currently in active development and is not yet available on the App Store.
