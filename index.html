AmiiNest

NFC Write & Collect

AmiiNest brings amiibo collection tracking, NFC scanning, and compatible NTAG215 tag writing together on your iPhone. Browse the amiibo catalog, organize your collection, and follow a guided four-step writing process using your own compatible data.

Browse & Search

Explore the amiibo catalog and find characters across different series.

Manage Your Collection

Track what you own, save favorites, keep a wishlist, and see which amiibo you're still missing.

Scan & Import

Scan your own amiibo using NFC to create backups, or import your compatible .bin backup files.

Guided NFC Writing

Write to blank NTAG215 tags in four guided steps, with support for resuming interrupted writing to the same tag.

Download AmiiNest for iPhone

Start with 5 free NFC writes. Additional write packs and a one-time Lifetime Unlimited purchase are available. No subscription required.
